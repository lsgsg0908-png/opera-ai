Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

OPERA AI collects the following information to provide and improve our service:

  • Account Information: Email address, encrypted password, payment information
  • Usage Data: Task history, token consumption, PC performance metrics
  • Screen Content: Screenshots taken during task execution (temporary, processed immediately)
  • System Information: OS version, hardware configuration, installed software list

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for: service operation, task execution, billing, customer support, and service improvement. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Task history is stored according to your plan: 7 days (Free/Basic), 30 days (Pro), 1 year (Enterprise). You may request deletion at any time.

4. Data Security

All communications are encrypted via TLS 1.3. API keys and credentials are stored encrypted locally. All agent actions are logged for audit purposes.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries: privacy@operaai.net
